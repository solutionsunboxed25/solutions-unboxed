About

Practical problem solving without unnecessary complexity.

Solutions Unboxed was created to provide reliable support to businesses that need someone who can understand a problem, learn the systems involved, organize information, communicate clearly, and help move the work toward a solution.

The business is being developed around real-world experience in technical troubleshooting, customer service, service coordination, documentation, research, scheduling, estimates, vendor communication, and day-to-day operations.
